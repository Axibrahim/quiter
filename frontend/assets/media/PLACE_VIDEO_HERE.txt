Place your glass-flower.mp4 in this folder.

the navbar and headline with a JS-driven crossfade loop (see
initHeroVideoCrossfade() in main.js): it fades in on first play, fades to
black just before it ends, then seamlessly restarts and fades back in.

If you already added this file for the previous frontend build, just copy
it over from that project's assets/media/ folder — same filename, same
purpose.
